✓ In-Scope Verification

  • • Product catalog rendering & immutable pricing.
  • • Real-time shopping cart mutations & bounds.
  • • Integer paise line subtotal & grand total math.
  • • Customer name validation & error recovery.
  • • Synchronous double-click duplicate protection.
  • • Unique simulated order reference generation.

✕ Out-of-Scope Boundaries

  • • Real payment gateway processing (Stripe, etc.).
  • • Persistent database storage (Postgres/Mongo).
  • • Backend servers, microservices & webhooks.
  • • User authentication & session cookies.

High Risk
Validation Integrity & State Retention
Missing customer name rejection, cart state preservation during errors, and quantity boundaries.
Priority 3
Medium Risk
Interactive Controls & Accuracy
Adding/removing products, enforcing quantity floor of 1, and confirmation text consistency.
Priority 4
Low Risk
Presentation & Basic Accessibility
Responsive layout changes, focus rings, and visual styling harmony across viewport widths.
